## Overview

FluxMintDigital maintains two complementary blogs. The **Studio Blog** explores architecture, apps, tools, frameworks, cognitive models, and behind-the-scenes development. The **Work-at-Home Equipment Blog** provides practical equipment reviews, product guides, and tools for people working from home. Together, they form one publishing ecosystem serving two different kinds of readers.
