# FluxMintDigital AAA Treatment Matrix

## Authority and status key

This reconciliation uses, in order: canonical Documents 01–06; authorized Human Decisions / Blocker Resolution; the AAA production manifest/checklist; approved-direction PNGs; then the current repository census. `VERIFIED-ASSET` means the file/component was inspected in the repository. It does not mean production-approved. External brand, cover and photographic DJ sources are conceptually approved but pending import, so they remain `NOT-YET-SEEN` as files without reopening their design decisions.

Treatments apply to future production use. Legacy assets marked KEEP remain as preserved evidence; that does not promote them to canonical production art.
