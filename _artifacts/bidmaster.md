## What it is

BidMaster is a local-first estimating workspace for independent contractors and small crews. It brings contractor-entered scope, measurements, quantities, costs, photos, estimates, and proposals into one working place that remains useful offline.

## Why it exists

An estimate has to carry a real job from early measurements and assumptions into something a contractor can review and a customer can understand. BidMaster exists to make that path easier to follow without hiding the inputs or depending on a hosted account for the core work.

## What makes it different

Calculations are deterministic and remain grounded in the information the contractor enters. Material, labor, access, safety, tax, and package totals can be reviewed before preparing customer-safe proposals and documents. Photos are job documentation—not automated analysis—and private contractor details remain separate from customer-facing output.

## Where it is now

BidMaster is on the Workshop workbench. Its estimating, proposal, cost-tracking, documentation, and offline foundations are being prepared and checked for a future release, but no public release or way to obtain it has been announced.
