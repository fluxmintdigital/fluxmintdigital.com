## What it is

Mint Pro is a publishing application DJ is building to turn structured manuscripts into consistent publication files. Authors work with the structure of the book while controlled templates and deterministic layout handle presentation, preview, and EPUB/PDF output.

## Why it exists

Book production can become a long negotiation with repeated formatting decisions. Mint Pro grew from a simpler question: what if the manuscript could remain structured while the publishing system applied those decisions reliably?

## What makes it different

It is not a blank canvas for decorating pages one at a time. The emphasis is on repeatable rules, clean templates, and dependable output from the same manuscript structure.

## Where it is now

Mint Pro is the current build on the Workshop workbench. It is still being shaped, and no release or availability details have been announced.
