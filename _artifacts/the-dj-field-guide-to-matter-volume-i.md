## What it is

*The DJ Field Guide to Matter* is an approachable, illustrated exploration of matter: the stuff around us, the stuff within us, and even the stuff we cannot see directly.

## Why it exists

“Stuff” is such an ordinary word that it is easy to stop wondering about it. This book begins with that everyday familiarity and turns it back into a reason to look closer.

## What makes it different

The Field Guide approach uses a consistent, structured journey, memorable illustrations, and humor to make foundational ideas inviting to curious readers of different ages. It is built for exploration without treating the subject as a list of facts to memorize.

Volume I is written and awaiting publication.
