## What it is

Architectural Thinking™ is a way of looking beneath visible outcomes for the relationships, constraints, patterns, and choices that helped produce them. Instead of stopping at “What happened?”, it asks, “What architecture produced this?”

## Why it exists

Problems rarely appear from nowhere. A difficult outcome may be the last visible expression of a structure that has been forming for some time. Making that structure easier to see can improve the questions we ask before we act.

## What makes it different

The framework is less interested in collecting isolated facts than in understanding how facts connect. It complements subject expertise; it does not replace it, and it does not promise that every complex situation has one simple explanation.

## Where it is now

This is the Studio’s maintained introduction to the framework. Its individual claims still have to earn support through examination; the framework does not count as evidence for itself.
