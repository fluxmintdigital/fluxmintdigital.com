## What participated in making you who you are?

The Personal Architecture Map is a 28-page instrument for examining the forces, inherited patterns, choices, tensions, and relationships that participate in a life. It is designed for personal reflection on paper or tablet and can be returned to more than once.

## Begin with a choice

The free [Choice Audit](/workshop/choice-audit/) is one complete doorway into the larger map. It asks what participated in a choice without turning your reflections into a result, score, or personality type.

## The larger architecture

The full instrument moves through Formation, Inheritance, Recognition, Choice, Alignment, Friction, Negative Geometry, Influence and Control, Becoming, and Personal Architecture. These are places to look—not stages to complete or answers the map supplies for you.

## What this instrument does not decide for you

It does not assign a type, score your life, diagnose you, uncover a hidden “real self,” or tell you what should change.

> **The Personal Architecture Map reveals relationships. It does not assign meaning on your behalf.**

## Built beside the book

The map is a companion to [*The Architecture of Being Human*](/library/architecture-series/the-architecture-of-being-human-volume-i/), not a prerequisite for reading it and not an answer key. Each can be explored on its own.
