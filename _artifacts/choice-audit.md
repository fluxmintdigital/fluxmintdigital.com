## Begin with one choice

It can be large or ordinary, recent or old. You do not need to know yet why you made it.

The Choice Audit is a free reflection instrument built around one question:

> **What participated in this choice?**

It makes room to notice desire, expectation, security, opportunity, identity, obligation, fear and avoidance, and possibility. Use only the forces that seem relevant. A blank space does not mean unfinished work.

## What it does—and does not do

The audit helps you preserve what you noticed and look at relationships in your own words. It does not score, diagnose, rank, assign a type, claim causation, or decide what your reflections mean.

> **A choice can belong to you without having originated only from you.**

## Where it is now

The browser-native instrument is on the Workshop workbench. It requires no email address, account, purchase, or personal information before use. A complete three-page printable version is available on this page too.
