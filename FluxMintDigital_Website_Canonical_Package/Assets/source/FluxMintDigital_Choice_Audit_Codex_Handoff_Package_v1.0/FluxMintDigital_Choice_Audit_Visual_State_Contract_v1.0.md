# Choice Audit — Visual State Contract v1.0

## Governing Principle
> **The interface should look increasingly inhabited as the reader works, never increasingly complete.**

## Arrival / Empty
Warm cream working surface with fine construction lines, subtle registration marks, restrained bronze-gold orientation, eight force names, and central Choice field.

Center:
**THE CHOICE**

> Begin with one choice.

The page should feel like architecture waiting for notation, not a form waiting for completion.

## Active
When a choice is entered, reader-created content becomes visually stronger than the printed scaffold.

Rule:
> **Reader-created content should visually outrank the printed/interface scaffold.**

No completion animation.

## Force Focus
Focused region gains:
- stronger text contrast
- slightly stronger boundary geometry
- writing surface
- prompt
- classifications
- influence controls

Do not turn it into a generic card.

## Inhabited Map
The map looks different because the reader changed it.

Blank regions remain legitimate.

> **Blank ≠ incomplete.**

No warning or incomplete styling for untouched regions.

## Relationship State
Reader-authored:
`Expectation ↔ Security`

Use thin architectural annotation. No inferred arrows or AI-detected relationship.

## Local Save
Use:
**Saving locally…**
then
**Saved on this device**

No cloud icon, sync checkmark, or success coloring.

## Temporal Shift
Then / After / Now changes composition from spatial mapping to temporal inspection.

Desktop may use:
**THEN → AFTER → NOW**

Arrow means chronology only.

## Evidence Lens
Heading:
**BEFORE YOU DECIDE WHAT IT MEANS**

Use a quieter inspection mode. No warning visuals.

Prompts:
- What supports this?
- What might challenge it?
- What else could explain it?
- What remains uncertain?

Hindsight check receives breathing room:
> **Am I reconstructing the decision differently because I know what happened afterward?**

## Reflection
Heading:
**WHAT DO YOU NOTICE?**

Never:
**YOUR RESULTS**

The system may summarize only the visitor's own entries. It may not interpret the person.

## Preservation
**What here appears worth preserving?**

Give it real writing space without positivity/success styling.

## Larger Architecture Reveal
Only after reflection, reveal:
01 Formation  
02 Inheritance  
03 Recognition  
**04 Choice**  
05 Alignment  
06 Friction  
07 Negative Geometry  
08 Influence / Control  
09 Becoming  
10 Personal Architecture

This may use restrained motion as a scale revelation, never a sales animation.

## Print State
Print must feel like a native physical Choice Audit artifact, not a browser screenshot.

No navigation/buttons/web chrome/shadows/hover affordances.

Preserve reader content, scaffold, marks, relationships, date, and footer:
**CHOICE AUDIT / FLUXMINTDIGITAL**

Must work in grayscale.

## Mobile State
Top:
**04 / CHOICE AUDIT**

**What participated in this choice?**

Compact force index:
Desire / Expectation / Security / Opportunity / Identity / Obligation / Fear & Avoidance / Possibility

Touched region may show a neutral indicator like:
`DESIRE ·`

Never:
`DESIRE ✓`

Selecting one expands inline.

Preferred control:
**View the whole map**

## Canonical Visual Dominance Hierarchy
1. Reader-created content
2. Governing question
3. Current focus
4. Structural relationships
5. Field labels
6. Construction geometry
7. Environmental texture

## Color Semantics
Bronze-gold = orientation / structural emphasis only.  
Charcoal = primary readable structure.  
Cream = working field.  
Near-black = threshold / environmental depth.

No red/green binary semantics. No meaning may depend only on color.

## Motion Semantics
Every animation must support one of:
- Reveal
- Focus
- Transition
- Return

Explicit prohibition:
> **Reader input itself should never trigger celebratory motion.**

## Digital Constitutional Invariant
> **The interface may reveal structure, preserve evidence, and reflect what the person entered. It must not quietly become the authority that tells the person what their structure means.**
